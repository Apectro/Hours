Zeitkonto for iOS

Write to us at REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL. Say which iPhone and which iOS version you have, and what you expected to happen. A person reads every message.

Zeitkonto has no account and no server, so we cannot see your data — please don't send a backup file unless we ask, and remove anything you would rather we didn't read.

Where are my hours stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server of ours. Your days, sites, crews and pay are included in your device's own backup (iCloud Backup, or a backup to a computer) like any app's data. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I make a backup, and restore it?

Settings › Backup and data › Share backup writes one readable JSON file with every day, holiday, site and setting. Keep it wherever you like — Files, iCloud Drive, an email to yourself.

To restore, open Settings › Backup and data › Restore from a backup and pick the file. Restoring replaces what is on the phone with what is in the file, and asks before it does.

I have a new phone. How do I take my hours with me?

If you move to the new iPhone from an iCloud Backup, from a computer backup or with Quick Start, Zeitkonto's data comes with it. If you set the new phone up fresh, make a backup on the old one (above), send the file to yourself, install Zeitkonto on the new phone and restore it there.

What does Zeitkonto Pro add?

Recording is free: the calendar, the day editor, your first site and its crew, earnings, the balance, reminders and backups. Pro adds exporting files (PDF, Excel, CSV — including the director's monthly sheet), the widgets and the Live Activity, more than one site, a second job, and editing a range of days at once. It is monthly, yearly, or a single purchase. Nothing you recorded is ever locked away if a subscription ends.

I paid, but Pro is locked. How do I restore my purchase?

A purchase belongs to your Apple Account, so it should appear by itself on every device signed in with it. If it doesn't, open Settings › Unlock Zeitkonto Pro and tap Restore. Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you paid with. There is no Zeitkonto account to log in to.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name › Subscriptions › Zeitkonto, and choose Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for. For a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com — Apple handles all payments and refunds.

Who can see my crew's names and my pay?

Nobody but you, unless you export a file. A site's sheet carries the crew's names and hours, never money; your earnings appear only on a personal timesheet you export with earnings switched on.
