Privacy Policy

Hours for iOS · last updated 26 August 2026

Hours does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no login, and no server operated by us. Your hours are stored on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you deliberately send it — by exporting a file, or by turning on iCloud sync, which puts your data in your own iCloud account and nowhere else.

This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What Hours stores, and where

Everything you record — days, hours, shifts, breaks, notes, tags, day types, holidays and your settings — is written to a private database inside the app on your device. It is not readable by other apps, and it is included in your device backup if you make one.

Hours has no server. We cannot see your data, and we could not retrieve it for you if you asked.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync in Settings › iCloud, your days, holidays and settings are stored in your own private iCloud storage, under your Apple Account. Only devices signed in as you can read it. It is Apple's service, governed by Apple's privacy policy, and no third party — including us — has access to it.

Sync is off until you switch it on, and you can switch it off again at any time.

Sync is not a backup. A day you delete is deleted on every device.

What we do not do

  • No analytics. Hours contains no analytics or telemetry of any kind. We do not know when or whether you open the app.
  • No tracking. Nothing about you is combined with data from other apps or websites, for advertising or for any other purpose.
  • No third-party code. The app contains no advertising networks, no crash reporters and no third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to create.
  • No selling or sharing. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to sell or share.

Location

Hours has an optional location field on a day. It is a piece of text that you type — a site name, an office, a client. The app never asks your device where you are and has no location permission.

Notifications

If you turn on the reminder in Settings, Hours schedules a local notification on your device to nudge you about days with nothing recorded. It is scheduled and delivered entirely on the device; no notification is sent through any server, and the app asks the system for permission only once you switch the reminder on.

Purchases

Hours Pro is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, exactly as every other purchase on your phone is. Hours is told one thing in return: whether it has been paid for. The app never sees your name, your email address or your payment details. There is no account to create, because a purchase belongs to your Apple Account.

Exports and backups

When you export a timesheet as CSV, Excel or PDF, or write a backup file, the file is created on your device and handed to the system share sheet. You choose where it goes — a file on the device, iCloud Drive, a message, an email. Hours does not upload it anywhere and has no knowledge of where you send it.

Children

Hours collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, portability and erasure normally require asking a company for a copy of what it holds. That does not apply here, because we hold nothing. In practical terms:

  • Access and portability — Settings › Backup and data writes a plain, readable JSON file containing everything the app holds.
  • Correction — every value is editable in the app.
  • Erasure — deleting the app removes its local database. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove the app's data from your Apple Account in the system Settings app, under your name › iCloud.
