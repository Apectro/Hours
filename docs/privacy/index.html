Privacy Policy

Zeitkonto for iOS · last updated 24 October 2026

Zeitkonto does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no login, and no server operated by us. Your hours, your sites, the names of your crew and your pay are stored on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you deliberately send it: by exporting a file and sharing it yourself, or, if you turn sync on, into your own private iCloud, which only your devices can read.

This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What Zeitkonto stores, and where

Everything you record — days, hours, shifts, breaks, notes, tags, day types, holidays, your working schedule and your settings — is written to a private database inside the app on your device. It is not readable by other apps, and it is included in your device backup if you make one.

Zeitkonto has no server. We cannot see your data, and we could not retrieve it for you if you asked.

Sites, crews and pay

Sites and crews. If you use sites, the app stores each site's name, the names you give the people on its crew, and who was there on which day and for how long. These are names you type; the app does not read your contacts and has no contacts permission. They stay on your device like everything else, and appear only on the sheets you choose to export.

Pay and earnings. If you enter an hourly rate, surcharges or a currency in Settings › Pay, they are stored on your device and used to work out what your own hours earned. Your pay is never written onto a site's sheet or anywhere about your crew, and it leaves the device only on a personal timesheet you export with earnings switched on.

Widgets, Live Activity and Siri

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets cannot open the app's database, so the app writes a small summary for them — today's, this week's and this month's figures, your balance, leave days and earnings, your current site and who was there last and why anyone was away — into a container shared only between Zeitkonto and its own widgets, on the same device. While the clock runs, a Live Activity shows it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Starting or stopping the clock with Siri or Shortcuts is handled by the app on the device. None of this leaves the device.

iCloud

From version 1.1, Zeitkonto can keep your data the same on your own devices through iCloud. It is off until you turn it on in Settings › iCloud, and it is a setting of each device: restoring a backup on another phone never switches it on there. Version 1.0 does not sync and does not use iCloud.

When sync is on, your days, holidays, sites, crews, site diaries (with their photos and signatures) and settings are kept in your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account, through Apple's CloudKit. Only your devices signed in to that account can read it. We have no access to it, no copy of it and no server in between; Apple stores it as it stores the rest of your iCloud data. Zeitkonto still collects nothing.

Sync is not a backup: a day you delete on one device is deleted on the others. Turning sync off stops the updates and deletes nothing, on the device or in iCloud. To remove what Zeitkonto keeps in iCloud, open the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Zeitkonto and delete its data.

Your device's own iCloud Backup, if you use it, includes the app's data as it does for every app.

What we do not do

  • No analytics. Zeitkonto contains no analytics or telemetry of any kind. We do not know when or whether you open the app.
  • No tracking. Nothing about you is combined with data from other apps or websites, for advertising or for any other purpose.
  • No third-party code. The app contains no advertising networks, no crash reporters and no third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to create.
  • No selling or sharing. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to sell or share.

Location

Location only if you turn on an arrival reminder for a site, and it never leaves your phone.

From version 1.2, a site can remind you to start the clock when you arrive and to stop it when you leave. It is off until you turn it on in that site's settings. Turning it on asks your device for your location once, to save where the site is: the site's latitude and longitude, and how close counts as arrived. That saved place is the only location Zeitkonto keeps. To notice you arriving while the app is closed, your device then watches the area around each such site and wakes Zeitkonto when you cross its edge, which is why it asks for location access “Always”. Zeitkonto does not track your movements, record where you have been, or keep any location history.

The saved place is stored with the site on your device, and goes wherever the site does: into a backup file you export, and into your own iCloud if you turned sync on. It is never sent to us or anyone else. The small map of the site in its settings is drawn by Apple Maps, which loads the map of that area from Apple as any map does. “Clear location” in the site's settings removes the place, and Settings › Zeitkonto › Location on your device lets you change or withdraw the permission at any time.

Without an arrival reminder, Zeitkonto never asks your device where you are. The optional location field on a day, and a site's name, are text you type.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders in Settings, Zeitkonto schedules local notifications on your device: a nudge about working days with nothing recorded, a note at the end of a site day that is not written yet, on the 1st of the month a note per site that last month's sheet is ready for the director, and — if you turned on a site's arrival reminder — a question when you reach or leave that site. They are scheduled and delivered entirely on the device; no notification is sent through any server, and the app asks the system for permission only once you switch a reminder on.

Purchases

This version of Zeitkonto sells nothing: there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If paid features are added later, payment will be handled entirely by the App Store, and this policy will be updated before then.

Exports and backups

When you export a timesheet or a site's sheet as CSV, Excel or PDF, or write a backup file, the file is created on your device and handed to the system share sheet. You choose where it goes — a file on the device, iCloud Drive, a message, an email. Zeitkonto does not upload it anywhere and has no knowledge of where you send it. A site's sheet carries the names and hours of the people on it and your name if you set one, so share it with the people it is meant for.

Children

Zeitkonto collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, portability and erasure normally require asking a company for a copy of what it holds. That does not apply here, because we hold nothing. In practical terms:

  • Access and portability — Settings › Backup and data writes a plain, readable JSON file containing everything the app holds.
  • Correction — every value is editable in the app, including the names on a crew.
  • Erasure — deleting the app removes its local database and the widgets' summary with it. If you turned sync on, delete the iCloud copy as described under iCloud above.
