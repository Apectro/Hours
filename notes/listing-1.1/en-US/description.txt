Zeitkonto is a work-hours calendar for the person who runs the site: write the day once for the whole crew, tell your boss what extra work is done, hand the director a clean monthly sheet, and see what your own hours earned.

No account. No sign-up. No server. Your hours, your sites and your crew's names stay on your phone, and nothing leaves it unless you export it or turn on sync to your own private iCloud.

ONE ENTRY FOR THE WHOLE CREW

• A site has its crew. Enter the day's times once and tick who was there.
• Someone came late or left at noon? Change their times only; everyone else keeps the site's.
• "Same as last day" fills today from the last day on the site in one tap.
• Mark who was off and why: sick, vacation, a day off or another site.
• Each site has its own month calendar, with how many were there each day.
• Rained off? Stop the whole crew for bad weather, all day or from a time.
• A site diary for each day: the weather, short notes and photos.

EXTRA WORK, REPORTED

Write down what the crew did beyond the contract, on the day: what was done, the material counted ("8 × emergency light, 25 m cable"), how long it took and who was on it, and photos. A job stays In progress until you tick it Done, and the client can sign for it on your phone. "Report to boss" sends what is done as PDF, Excel or CSV, written in your boss's language. Quantities only: there are no prices in it.

THE DIRECTOR'S MONTHLY SHEET

At the start of the month Zeitkonto reminds you, per site, that last month's sheet is ready. It lists everyone, one group of columns per person, a big crew over several pages: PDF, Excel or CSV, previewed exactly as it will print. If you want it, each person signs their month on your phone first. Money never appears on a site sheet.

WHAT YOU EARNED

Set an hourly rate and the calendar leads with what you earned: overtime after a normal day or after your week, Saturday, Sunday, holiday and night surcharges, paid days off at your rate, and sick pay at a percentage. A raise starts from a date, so earlier months keep the rate they were worked at. Earnings are private to you and never shown about your crew.

A CALENDAR FIRST

The main screen is the month, coloured by day type, with the totals underneath. Tap a day to see it, tap again to edit it. A past working day with nothing written gets a dashed outline, so you notice the gap now.

HOURS THE WAY YOURS WORK

• Start and end times; an end before the start is a night shift
• Breaks as a length or as clock times
• Split shifts, and more than one job, each with its own week
• Your contracted week per weekday, changing from a date when your contract does
• Vacation, sick leave, time off in lieu and your own day types
• Your leave allowance and what is left of it
• Public holidays for Germany (by state), Austria, Switzerland (by canton), Croatia, Slovenia, Italy, France, Spain, Portugal, the Netherlands and Poland

THE BALANCE, FROM YOUR FIRST DAY

worked + paid absence − expected + adjustment

Counted from your first recorded day, so starting mid-year doesn't put you hundreds of hours behind. Insights covers the day, week, month and year, earnings first, with a Year in review.

WATCH, WIDGETS AND LIVE ACTIVITY

An Apple Watch app with today's hours and Clock in / Clock out. Widgets for today, the week, the month and your current site. While the clock runs, a Live Activity shows it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Clock in and out from Control Center, the Action Button, Siri or Shortcuts.

BUILT NOT TO LOSE YOUR DATA

Days are stored as dates, so a time zone or a clock change can't reshape them. Close a month you have handed in and nothing can change it by accident. Backup writes a plain file holding everything: a file you keep, not a service you trust.

FREE, ALL OF IT

Everything is free: sites and crews, extra work and the report to your boss, the director's sheet, earnings, the Apple Watch app, the widgets and the Live Activity. No account, no ads.
