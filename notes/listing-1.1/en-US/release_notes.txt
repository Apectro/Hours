EXTRA WORK FOR YOUR BOSS
• Write down extra work on a site, on the day: what was done, the material counted, how long it took and who was on it, and photos.
• A job stays In progress until you tick it Done.
• The client can sign for it on your phone.
• "Report to boss" sends what is done as PDF, Excel or CSV, written in your boss's language. Quantities only, no prices.

ON SITE
• A site diary for each day: the weather, short notes and photos.
• Rained off: stop the whole crew for bad weather, all day or from a time.
• Crew sign-off, if you want it: each person signs their month on your phone, and a change after signing shows on the director's sheet.

YOUR TIME
• Public holidays for Germany (by state), Austria, Switzerland (by canton), Croatia, Slovenia, Italy, France, Spain, Portugal, the Netherlands and Poland.
• Your leave allowance, what is carried over and what is left.
• Time off in lieu as a day type of its own.
• Year in review: your year in figures, to look back on or share.

WATCH AND SHORTCUTS
• An Apple Watch app: today's hours, the month, and Clock in / Clock out. A complication for the watch face.
• Clock in and out from Control Center or the Action Button.
• iCloud sync between your own devices, if you turn it on. Still no account.

AND
• A welcome that sets up your report language, crew and pay, and a short guided tour of the app.
