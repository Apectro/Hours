Zeitkonto is fully functional without paying. Recording hours, the calendar, the day editor, the first site with its crew, earnings, the balance, reminders and the JSON backup are free and unlimited. Nothing recorded is ever withheld behind a purchase.

WHERE PRO IS
Zeitkonto Pro unlocks exporting files (PDF / Excel / CSV), the widgets and the Live Activity, more than one active site, a second job, and editing a range of days at once. The paywall is the first row in Settings ("Unlock Zeitkonto Pro"), and it also opens from any of those features, for example Calendar > ... > Export > Share, or Sites > + for a second site. The paywall has a "Restore" button.

SANDBOX PRODUCTS
  app.zeitkonto.pro.monthly   auto-renewable, 1 month
  app.zeitkonto.pro.yearly    auto-renewable, 1 year (same subscription group)
  app.zeitkonto.pro.lifetime  non-consumable
All three can be bought with a sandbox account.

NO ACCOUNT, NO NETWORK
There is no account and no login, so no demo account is needed. The app makes no network requests except to the App Store for purchases. This version does not offer iCloud sync. There is no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no third-party code.

PERMISSIONS
Notifications only, and only when the user turns a reminder on. The optional "location" field on a day is text the user types: the app never asks for location permission and links no location framework.

TO SEE THE MAIN FEATURES QUICKLY
Sites > + > enter a name > Crew > add two names. Back on the site, tick who was there; the day is written to the calendar too. Settings > Pay > enter an hourly rate to see earnings on the calendar and in Insights.

TESTFLIGHT BUILDS
TestFlight builds unlock Pro for testers. That unlock is compiled out of the build submitted for review (built with TESTER_UNLOCK off), so the paywall and purchases behave exactly as for a customer.
