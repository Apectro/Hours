Zeitkonto is a work-hours calendar for the person who runs the site: write the day once for the whole crew, hand the director a clean monthly sheet, and see what your own hours earned.

No account. No sign-up. No server. Your hours, your sites and your crew's names stay on your phone, and nothing leaves it unless you export it.

ONE ENTRY FOR THE WHOLE CREW

• A site has its crew. Enter the day's times once and tick who was there.
• Someone came late or left at noon? Change their times only; everyone else keeps the site's.
• "Same as last day" fills today from the last day on the site in one tap.
• Each site has its own month calendar, with how many were there each day.
• Starting a new site? Pick people from your other crews instead of typing them again.
• Paid breaks are a setting per job, because employers don't agree on them.

THE DIRECTOR'S MONTHLY SHEET

At the start of the month Zeitkonto reminds you, per site, that last month's sheet is ready. It lists everyone, one group of columns per person, with your name at the top: PDF, Excel or CSV, previewed exactly as it will print. Money never appears on a site sheet.

WHAT YOU EARNED

Set an hourly rate and the calendar leads with what you earned: overtime after a normal day or after your week, Saturday, Sunday and holiday and night surcharges, and paid days off at your rate. A raise starts from a date, so the months before it keep the rate they were worked at. Earnings are private to you and never shown about your crew.

A CALENDAR FIRST

The main screen is the month, coloured by day type, with the totals underneath. Tap a day to see it, tap again to edit it. A past working day with nothing written on it gets a dashed outline, so you notice the gap now rather than at the end of the month.

HOURS THE WAY YOURS WORK

• Start and end times; an end before the start is a night shift
• Breaks as a length or as clock times
• Split shifts, and more than one job, each with its own week
• Your contracted week per weekday, changing from a date when your contract does
• Vacation, sick leave, public holidays and your own day types
• Every field behind a switch: what you turn off disappears

THE BALANCE, FROM YOUR FIRST DAY

worked + paid absence − expected + adjustment

Counted from the first day you recorded, so installing mid-year doesn't start you hundreds of hours behind. Worked time and paid absence are always shown separately. Insights covers today, the week, the month and the year, with earnings first.

WIDGETS AND LIVE ACTIVITY

Today, This month, This week and your current site on the Home Screen, with "Same as yesterday" right on the site widget. While the clock runs, a Live Activity shows it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a stop button. Siri and Shortcuts can start and stop it too.

BUILT NOT TO LOSE YOUR DATA

Days are stored as dates, so a time zone or a clock change can't reshape them. Close a month you have handed in and nothing can change it by accident. Backup and restore writes a plain JSON file holding everything you entered: a file you keep, not a service you trust.

ZEITKONTO PRO

Recording is free and stays free: the calendar, the day editor, your first site with its crew, earnings, the balance, reminders and the backup. Nothing you recorded is ever held back.

Pro is for producing: sheets and timesheets as PDF, Excel or CSV, the widgets and the Live Activity, more than one site, a second job, and editing a range of days at once. Monthly, yearly, or once for good. No account and no login: a purchase belongs to your Apple Account.
